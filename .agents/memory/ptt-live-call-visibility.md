---
name: PTT live call visibility
description: The administrator visibility boundary for direct and group PTT calls.
---

Administrator PTT views show call status and participants only; they do not provide live audio monitoring.

**Why:** the user chose status-only visibility for the technician call pop-up.

**How to apply:** Provide ringing, connected, declined, and unavailable states plus participant names. Do not add administrator listen-in audio.
